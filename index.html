Biography

The story behind the work

Short version: no university position, no institutional funding, and a body of work that anyone can check.

Kallol Chakrabarti is an unaffiliated independent researcher based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. He is self-taught, learning through YouTube and HarvardX online courses, and has built a career on one idea: a person does not need an institution's permission to create knowledge or to protect it.

In 2025 he published 158 sole-authored, DOI-indexed research papers in a single calendar year across Zenodo, DeSci Labs, SSRN and other open repositories. That output made him a record holder of both the India Book of Records and the Asia Book of Records.

Today he serves as a Global Peer Reviewer at ScienceOpen. He was invited by the University of Sussex to assess research novelty indicators, and he participated in the SAGER@10 survey by EASE and GENDRO on sex and gender equity in publishing.

Alongside the research, he is a web developer who has built more than 550 websites. He now brings that experience to clients: record-application documentation, professional biographies and research for creators. His own record journey, fully documented, is the best proof that he knows what a successful application needs.
