# Kallol Chakrabarti | Record Holder, Peer Reviewer & Independent Researcher

**Live site:** https://helixoriginator.github.io/kallol-chakrabarti-record-holder-biography/

A single-page biography and portfolio of **Kallol Chakrabarti** (Helix Originator), an independent researcher, author and web developer from Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. He is a record holder of both the **Asia Book of Records** and the **India Book of Records**, and a **Global Peer Reviewer at ScienceOpen**.

The page documents his achievements with linked evidence and presents the services he offers to clients.

## Highlights

- Record holder of both the Asia Book of Records and the India Book of Records
- 158 sole-authored, DOI-indexed research papers published in 2025
- 275+ published DOIs, 100+ original conceptual frameworks, 25 registered copyrights
- Global Peer Reviewer at ScienceOpen
- 550+ websites built (300 on Netlify, 238 repositories on GitHub)
- Featured on India News (television telecast, 22 November 2026, 5:30 AM IST)
